About ByteMind Club

ByteMind isn’t just a tech club—it’s a movement. Born inside DPSK, it’s where students become creators, coders, and demo masters. We believe tech should be fun, visual, and accessible to everyone.

From QR scanners to face detection, ByteMind sessions are built to spark curiosity and confidence. No jargon. No gatekeeping. Just pure innovation, live demos, and a community that glows with creativity.

Key Features

⚡ Live Demos: QR, Color, Face Detection
📊 Q/A and Polls Every Session
🧠 Beginner-Friendly Code & Hands-On Activities
📝 Easy To Understand Notes and PPTs
🤝 Inclusive, No-Jargon Learning Environment

🎓 Who Can Join ByteMind?

ByteMind Club currently runs sessions for 9th Grade students at Delhi Public School Kalinga.

We’re actively expanding to include 6th to 12th Grade students—so whether you're just starting out or already building bots, ByteMind is your launchpad.
