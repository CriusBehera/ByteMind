🚀 About ByteMind Club

ByteMind isn’t just a tech club—it’s a movement. Born inside Delhi Public School Kalinga, it’s where students become creators, coders, and demo masters. We believe tech should be fun, visual, and accessible to everyone.

From QR scanners to face detection, ByteMind sessions are built to spark curiosity and confidence. No jargon. No gatekeeping. Just pure innovation, live demos, and a community that glows with creativity.
